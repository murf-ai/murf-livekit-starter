Title: e-NAM farmer registration and benefits
Source: https://www.enam.gov.in/web/stakeholders-Involved/farmers

e-NAM is a pan-India electronic trading portal connecting existing APMC mandis.
Farmers can register through the e-NAM portal, mobile application, or at an e-NAM
mandi. The official farmer page says registration has no fee. It lists details such
as name, address, date of birth, mobile number, bank details, a bank passbook or
cheque leaf, and government identity proof. Stated benefits include transparent
bidding, access to more markets and buyers, mandi price and arrival information,
quality-based price discovery through assaying, and online payment. Current rules
and eligibility should be confirmed on the cited official page or with the APMC.
