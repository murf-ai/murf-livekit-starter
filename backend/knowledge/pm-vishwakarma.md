Title: PM Vishwakarma implementation guidelines
Source: https://www.dcmsme.gov.in/PMV-Ebook/PMV.pdf

PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme supporting artisans and craftspeople who
work with their hands and tools in the trades listed by the scheme. Support described
in the official implementation guidelines includes recognition through a certificate
and ID card, skill upgrading, a toolkit incentive, credit support, incentives for
digital transactions, and marketing support. Registration and eligibility must be
checked through the official PM Vishwakarma process because scheme rules can change.
Do not promise approval, a loan, a payment, or eligibility; the responsible authority
makes those decisions.
